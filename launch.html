Open AR

AR is a private desktop accounts receivable project for authorized internal use. It is not a web application and this page does not launch software on your computer.

Current availability

The desktop application is under development and is not yet available to launch. Initial QuickBooks integration work is performed with a locally installed research tool by the authorized Operator.

If you have just authorized a connection, return to the local tool that started authorization and check its result. Visiting this page is not confirmation that authorization succeeded.

Need access?

Contact the person or organization managing your AR installation through your existing business contact channel. There is no public signup or download on this website.
